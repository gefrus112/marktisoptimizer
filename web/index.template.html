Safety & rejected requests

No “hardware ban” promise. No exploit tricks. No hidden diagnostic disabling. The app writes local JSON and keeps backups.

Safety

Performance-only local settings.

No DLL injection, process hooks, memory reads/writes, executable patching, fullbright, x-ray, building removal, anti-cheat bypass, gameplay automation, or Roblox telemetry/Crashpad/Backtrace disabling. The app also sends no analytics of its own.

For real in-game FPS/ping, use Roblox’s built-in Performance Stats / Debug Stats. The HUD shows targets and system hints, not memory-read game values.
FAQ

Common questions

Does this inject a DLL into Roblox?

No. There is no injector. The bootstrapper writes local ClientAppSettings.json files and launches Roblox through the normal protocol.

Can it disable Crashpad, Backtrace, or screenshot uploads?

No. Those hidden diagnostic/abuse-reporting flags are not allowlisted and are intentionally not written. The app has no telemetry of its own.

Does the legacy FPS flag unlock 240+ FPS?

Not reliably. DFIntTaskSchedulerTargetFps is not on the current baseline allowlist and may be ignored. Use Roblox Settings > Maximum Framerate for supported FPS changes.

What are graphics tiers 0–21?

They map to the allowlisted DFIntDebugFRMQualityLevelOverride flag. 0 is automatic, 1–6 are low, and 7–21 are high/legacy micro levels. Roblox may change this behavior.

What does “reduce building lag” mean?

It lowers geometry LOD distance, grass distance, textures, MSAA, and motion through local settings. It does not remove buildings or change collision.

Can it remove buildings or add fullbright?

No. Those are exploit-style visual changes and are rejected.
